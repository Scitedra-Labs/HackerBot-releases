## English

HackerBot Defense Lab is a Hebrew defensive cyber lab: practice rooms, desktop and network simulations, and role paths, with no attack how-tos. The content teaches defense only: no exploit code, no malicious payloads and no attack recipes.

### What's inside

- Rooms and XP: a briefing, tasks, a simulation and a recap in every room
- Role paths: SOC, AppSec, networking and fundamentals
- Simulations: simulated Windows/Linux desktops, a defender terminal, a network map and alert triage
- Topics: passwords and policy, SOC triage, phishing and threat modeling
- Progress is saved in the browser, and in the cloud if you sign in

### Installing on Android

1. Open the [Releases page](https://github.com/Scitedra-Labs/HackerBot-releases/releases) **on your phone** and download the APK.
2. Open the file. If prompted, allow installs from this source.
3. Tap **Install**.

First Android beta: a test build (debug-signed) with internet permission only.
